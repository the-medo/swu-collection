# Crossfire logo concepts

Proposed adaptations of the original SWUBASE logo, designed by the
project owner's girlfriend. The source artwork is
[`logo-light-theme.svg`](../../logo-light-theme.svg). All concepts retain its
original Grogu paths. The latest set removes all lettering:

- **04 — Compact:** Grogu and the small crossed sabers, without a border.
- **05 — Longer blades:** wider crossed sabers across the robe, without a border.
- **06 — Oversized blades:** larger sabers with bright cores extending below Grogu.
- **07 — Blades behind:** tall sabers masked behind the original illustration.
- **08 — Round badge:** longer blades inside a circular frame.
- **09 — Framed badge:** small sabers in a compact rounded rectangular frame.

The earlier concepts also preserve the SWU lettering and original front frame:

- **01 — Wordmark:** replace “base” with an original Crossfire wordmark.
- **02 — Crossed blades:** add two small blades below Grogu's face.
- **03 — Card fan:** add two card outlines behind the front frame.

Each has a light-background and dark-background SVG, with no font or raster
dependencies. SVG masks provide transparent gaps around the blades, behind
Grogu and behind the card fan. The original illustration and
new details remain separate groups for editing.

Open [preview.html](preview.html) to compare the concepts and download assets.
With worktree Vite running, the preview is available at
`/src/assets/crossfire/concepts/preview.html`. It includes a background switch
and samples at 64, 40 and 24 CSS pixels; the earlier concepts are in a collapsible
section. These are design candidates; the app
uses **05 — Longer blades**, copied to [../logo-light.svg](../logo-light.svg) and
[../logo-dark.svg](../logo-dark.svg). The other concepts remain here for reference.
